## 1. The Problem

Expressmats runs 20+ ecommerce websites, and that number is growing. Every
product on every site needs good images and SEO-ready text. Sourcing or
shooting images and writing search-optimised copy for each product by hand is
slow, and it does not scale across a portfolio this size. You want a faster,
repeatable way to populate product pages, using a reference ecommerce site as
the starting point.

## 2. What We Propose

A pilot run of an AI-driven product content service. You give us a reference
ecommerce website. Our AI agent pulls the product images from that site,
generates fresh, similar images for your own products, and writes an
SEO-optimised writeup for each product. You receive images and copy ready to
go onto your website.

We run this on **1-2 of your websites first**, so you can judge the quality
of the output before committing to anything larger.

**In short, you will get:**

- AI-generated product images, modelled on a reference site, for each product
- An SEO-optimised writeup for each product
- Images and copy delivered ready for your website
- All AI and API generation costs covered by us, included in the price

## 3. Scope Summary

The pilot covers up to **1-2 websites**. For every product on those sites we
produce similar product images and a matching SEO writeup. If you would like,
we can also build the full website around the generated images and writeups
as an optional add-on (see Deliverables).
