## Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning
[BRIEF DESCRIPTION, e.g. an AI development engagement] (the "Purpose") and
expect to share confidential information for that Purpose.

## 1. Confidential Information

1.1 **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed
by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"),
in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential. It includes business plans, client and
customer data, pricing, technical information, source code, prompts, models,
datasets, and the existence and content of the Parties' discussions.

1.2 Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without using the
Confidential Information; or
(d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

## 2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party will:

2.1 use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

2.2 not disclose it to any third party except to its personnel or advisors
who need it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at
least as protective as this Agreement;

2.3 protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own
confidential information, and not less than reasonable care; and

2.4 not use it to train, fine-tune, or improve any publicly available AI or
machine-learning model.

## 3. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law or a court to disclose Confidential
Information, it may do so, but will, where lawful, give the Disclosing Party
prompt notice and reasonable assistance to limit the disclosure.

## 4. No Licence or Obligation

4.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No
licence or intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement.

4.2 This Agreement does not oblige either Party to enter into any further
agreement or transaction.

## 5. Term & Return

5.1 This Agreement applies to Confidential Information disclosed for **[2]
years** from the date above.

5.2 The confidentiality obligations survive for **[3] years** from the date
of disclosure. Trade secrets remain protected for as long as they are trade
secrets.

5.3 On written request, the Receiving Party will return or destroy the
Confidential Information, except copies it must retain by law or in routine
backups, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 6. No Warranty

Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party does not
warrant its accuracy or completeness.

## 7. Remedies

The Parties agree that a breach of this Agreement may cause harm that money
alone cannot remedy, and that the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief
in addition to any other remedy.
