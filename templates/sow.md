## 5. Acceptance Process

1. The Service Provider notifies the Client when a deliverable is ready for
   review.
2. The Client has **[5] business days** to review against the acceptance
   criteria in Section 2 and either accept or provide written, specific
   feedback.
3. If no response is received within the review window, the deliverable is
   deemed accepted.
4. Feedback within the included revision count is addressed at no extra cost.
   Work beyond the included revisions, or feedback outside the agreed
   acceptance criteria, is handled as a Change Request.
