# Service Agreement

## Recitals

A. The Service Provider provides artificial intelligence consulting,
development, and automation services.

B. The Client wishes to engage the Service Provider for such services, and
the Service Provider agrees to provide them, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Services"** means the work described in any Scope of Work.

1.2 **"Scope of Work"** or **"SOW"** means a document signed by both Parties
describing specific deliverables, timelines, and fees, attached as an
Annexure and incorporated into this Agreement.

1.3 **"Deliverables"** means the items the Service Provider is to deliver
under a SOW.

1.4 **"Confidential Information"** means non-public information disclosed by
one Party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a
reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

1.5 **"Client Materials"** means data, content, credentials, and other
materials the Client provides for the Services.

1.6 **"Pre-Existing IP"** means intellectual property owned by a Party before
the Effective Date, or developed outside this Agreement, including the
Service Provider's tools, frameworks, libraries, code components, and
know-how.

1.7 **"AI Services"** means third-party artificial intelligence or large
language model services used to perform the Services.

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 The Service Provider will provide the Services described in each SOW.

2.2 No work is binding unless set out in a SOW signed by both Parties. The
first SOW is attached as **Annexure A**.

2.3 Any change to a SOW must be made by a written Change Request signed by
both Parties, as set out in the relevant SOW.

## 3. Term

3.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until completed
or terminated under Clause 12.

3.2 Each SOW runs for the period stated in it.

## 4. Fees & Payment

4.1 The Client will pay the fees set out in each SOW.

4.2 Invoices are payable within **[7]** days of the invoice date by bank
transfer to the account notified by the Service Provider.

4.3 **Advance.** Work begins after the Client pays the advance stated in the
SOW. The advance is non-refundable once work has begun, except as required by
law.

4.4 **Late payment.** Overdue amounts carry interest at **[1.5]% per month**
(or the maximum permitted by law, if lower) from the due date until paid. The
Service Provider may suspend the Services on [7] days' written notice of
non-payment.

4.5 **Third-party costs.** AI Services usage, hosting, paid tools, and
licences are [billed to the Client at cost / paid directly by the Client].
These are not included in the fees unless the SOW says so.

## 5. Taxes

5.1 **GST.** All fees are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax and other
indirect taxes. As at the Effective Date the Service Provider is **not
registered under GST** and therefore does not charge GST. If the Service
Provider becomes liable to register for GST, applicable GST will be charged
on invoices issued from the date of registration, and the Client will pay it
in addition to the fees.

5.2 **TDS.** If the Client is required to deduct tax at source under the
Income-tax Act, 1961, it may do so and will provide the Service Provider with
a TDS certificate within the statutory period. The Service Provider's PAN is
**[PAN]**.

5.3 Each Party bears its own income tax.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 **Client Materials** remain owned by the Client. The Client grants the
Service Provider a licence to use them solely to perform the Services.

6.2 **Deliverables.** On receipt of **full payment** for the relevant SOW,
the Service Provider assigns to the Client all intellectual property rights
in the custom Deliverables created specifically for the Client under that
SOW. This assignment is made under the Copyright Act, 1957 and other
applicable law.

6.3 **Pre-Existing IP.** Pre-Existing IP remains owned by the Party that owns
it. Where a Deliverable incorporates the Service Provider's Pre-Existing IP,
the Service Provider grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive,
non-transferable licence to use that Pre-Existing IP as part of the
Deliverable. The Service Provider may continue to use its Pre-Existing IP and
general know-how, skills, and experience without restriction.

6.4 Until full payment is received, all IP in the Deliverables remains with
the Service Provider.

6.5 **Portfolio rights.** The Service Provider may describe the engagement
and display non-confidential Deliverables in its portfolio and marketing,
unless the Client objects in writing.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party will keep the other's Confidential Information confidential,
use it only to perform or receive the Services, and protect it with
reasonable care.

7.2 This does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the
receiving Party, was already known to it, is independently developed, or must
be disclosed by law.

7.3 This clause survives termination for **[3] years**. Trade secrets are
protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 Where the Service Provider processes personal data on the Client's behalf,
it acts as a Data Processor and the Client acts as the Data Fiduciary under
the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8.2 The Service Provider will process personal data only on the Client's
documented instructions, apply reasonable security safeguards, and assist the
Client with data-principal requests so far as reasonably practicable.

8.3 The Client confirms it has the lawful basis and consents needed for the
Service Provider to process the personal data contemplated by the SOW.

8.4 The Service Provider may use sub-processors, including AI Services
providers (see Clause 9), and remains responsible for their compliance with
this clause.

8.5 On termination, the Service Provider will delete or return personal data
at the Client's choice, except where retention is required by law.

## 9. AI-Specific Terms

9.1 **Third-party AI Services.** The Services may use third-party AI Services
(for example, providers of large language models). Their availability,
pricing, model versions, and terms are outside the Service Provider's
control. The Service Provider is not liable for changes, deprecations, or
outages of third-party AI Services.

9.2 **No warranty on AI output.** AI-generated output is probabilistic. It may
be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or "hallucinated". The Service Provider
does not warrant that AI output is accurate, fit for a particular purpose, or
free of error. AI output is not legal, medical, financial, or other
professional advice.

9.3 **Client review obligation.** The Client is responsible for human review
and validation of AI output before relying on it or putting it into
production, and for ensuring its use complies with laws applicable to the
Client's industry.

9.4 **Training data.** The Service Provider will not knowingly submit the
Client's Confidential Information or personal data to AI Services that use
inputs to train publicly available models, and will use available settings
to opt out of such training, where the Client's data is involved.

9.5 **Inputs and prompts.** Prompts, configurations, and instructions created
specifically for the Client under a SOW are treated as custom Deliverables
under Clause 6. General prompting techniques and methods remain the Service
Provider's Pre-Existing IP and know-how.

9.6 **Acceptable use.** The Client will not use the Deliverables or AI
Services for any unlawful, deceptive, or harmful purpose, or in breach of a
third-party AI provider's usage policies.

## 10. Warranties & Disclaimer

10.1 Each Party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this
Agreement.

10.2 The Service Provider warrants that it will perform the Services with
reasonable skill and care.

10.3 Except as expressly stated, the Services and Deliverables are provided
"as is". To the extent permitted by law, the Service Provider disclaims all
other warranties, express or implied, including merchantability and fitness
for a particular purpose.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or
consequential loss, or loss of profit, revenue, data, or goodwill.

11.2 The Service Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement
and any SOW is limited to the **total fees paid by the Client under the SOW
giving rise to the claim**.

11.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by
law, including liability for fraud or wilful misconduct.

## 12. Termination

12.1 **For convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or a SOW
on **[15]** days' written notice.

12.2 **For cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately on written notice
if the other materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach
within [10] days of notice, or becomes insolvent.

12.3 **Effect.** On termination, the Client will pay for all Services
performed and accepted, and for work in progress, up to the termination date.
Clauses 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, and 14 survive termination.

## 13. General

13.1 **Independent contractor.** The Service Provider is an independent
contractor. Nothing creates employment, partnership, or agency between the
Parties.

13.2 **Non-solicitation.** During the term and for [12] months after, neither
Party will directly solicit for employment any individual of the other Party
who worked on the Services, except through a general public job posting.

13.3 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused
by events beyond its reasonable control, including internet or AI Services
outages, acts of government, or natural events.

13.4 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent to the email addresses
or registered addresses of the Parties. Email notice is effective on
confirmed delivery.

13.5 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the
other's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

13.6 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement and its SOWs are the entire
agreement between the Parties and supersede all prior discussions and
proposals.

13.7 **Amendment.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both
Parties.

13.8 **Severability.** If a clause is held invalid, the rest of the Agreement
remains in effect.

13.9 **Waiver.** A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it.

13.10 **Counterparts & e-signature.** This Agreement may be signed in
counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

## 14. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, including the Indian
Contract Act, 1872.

14.2 The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith
discussion for [15] days.

14.3 If unresolved, the dispute will be referred to arbitration by a sole
arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and
venue of arbitration is **[CITY]**, the language is English, and the
arbitrator's award is final and binding.

14.4 Subject to arbitration, the courts at **[CITY, STATE]** have exclusive
