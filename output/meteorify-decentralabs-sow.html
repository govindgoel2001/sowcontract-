01

The objective

Give DecentraLabs one secure sales command centre that replaces the patchwork of CRMs and spreadsheets, and have it live within four working days.

Phase 1 is the sales engine, the part of the business currently holding back revenue. By the end of the build, the team logs in to a single place where every lead, call, message, payment and KPI lives together, with ad leads flowing in on their own and the floor ranked in real time. Phases 2 and 3 build on top of it later; only Phase 1 is in scope here.

02

What we deliver

Each item below states what it is, how you confirm it is done, and how many rounds of changes are included.

D1Access & roles

  • Secure multi-user login. No shared or open access.
  • Role-based access for Founder / Admin, Closer and Setter, each role seeing and doing only what it should.
  • Admin view across the whole floor; reps scoped to their own leads and KPIs.
Acceptance criteria

Each of the three roles logs in and sees exactly its permitted screens and data in a live test; a setter cannot view another rep's leads.

Revisions included

2 rounds

D2Leads & pipeline

  • Lead card with contact, source, priority, follower count, budget, owner, status, notes and full activity history.
  • Kanban pipeline with configurable stages (your milestones / qualified / cash-collected).
  • Lead-source attribution, auto-tagging source and UTM on entry.
  • Global search, filters, saved views, bulk actions and round-robin assignment to reps.
Acceptance criteria

A new lead can be created, moved across all stages, assigned, filtered and found by search, with every change recorded in its activity history.

Revisions included

2 rounds

D3Integrations & data migration

  • Meta / Facebook Ads webhook, so new ad leads flow in automatically with no manual entry.
  • Click-to-call dialer (VOIP / IVR) from the lead card, with call recording and transcript saved on the lead.
  • WhatsApp send to a lead directly from the CRM via OpenWA.
  • CSV import with de-duplication to port existing GoHighLevel / Iron Forge data, plus CSV export.
Acceptance criteria

A test ad lead arrives via webhook; a test call places, records and transcribes; a WhatsApp message sends from a lead; and a sample CSV imports with duplicates flagged.

Revisions included

2 rounds

D4Payments & deals

  • Deal value with split / partial payments, tracking each tranche (e.g. a 50,000 deal with 30,000 collected).
  • Date-based reminder for the next payment split.
  • Splits auto-feed the KPIs (cash collected, pending) with no double entry.
  • Contract value, cash collected, pending / outstanding and deals closed.
Acceptance criteria

A deal recorded with a partial payment updates cash-collected and pending automatically, and a split reminder appears on its due date.

Revisions included

2 rounds

D5KPIs, Arena & graphs

  • Arena leaderboard, a gamified per-rep ranking that drives the floor.
  • Graphical dashboards with daily trend graphs, not just monthly totals.
  • KPIs auto-computed from activity (calls, bookings, payments) per rep and per team: call rate, connected, booking rate, no-show, deals closed, close rate, revenue, cash collected, pending, collect rate, churn, leaderboard rank and trends.
Acceptance criteria

Logging calls, bookings and payments updates the matching KPIs and the leaderboard order in real time, with daily, weekly and monthly trend views available.

Revisions included

2 rounds

D6Platform & deployment

  • Multi-client command centre to run several clients' sales from one place.
  • Mobile-responsive across laptop, tablet and phone.
  • Hosted on a DecentraLabs subdomain, secured with SSL, on your own database.
  • Live deployment and a walkthrough handover call.
Acceptance criteria

The system is reachable on the agreed subdomain over HTTPS, usable on a phone, and demonstrated end-to-end on the handover call.

Revisions included

2 rounds

04

The four-day build

Day numbers are offsets from the confirmed start, which begins once the advance is paid and the access in Section 06 is in hand.

01Foundation

Database, auth & data migration

Database and schema stood up, secure login and role-based access live, and a full import of your existing CRM data with de-duplication. Your data is in the new system from day one.

02Pipeline

Leads, pipeline & payments

Lead cards, configurable Kanban stages, source attribution, search, filters, saved views, round-robin assignment, and the deals and split-payment tracking that feeds the KPIs.

03Integrations

Ads, dialer & WhatsApp

Meta Ads webhook wired in, click-to-call with recording and transcripts, WhatsApp send from the lead card, and CSV export. The Arena leaderboard and KPI dashboards come online.

04Launch

Testing, mobile polish & handover

End-to-end testing across pipeline, payments, KPIs and integrations, mobile-responsive polish, deployment on your subdomain with SSL, and a live walkthrough handover.

05

How sign-off works

  • We tell you when Phase 1 is ready for review.
  • You have 3 business days to check it against the acceptance criteria in Section 02 and either accept it or send written, specific feedback.
  • If we hear nothing within that window, Phase 1 is treated as accepted.
  • Feedback within the included revision rounds is handled at no extra cost. Anything beyond that, or outside the agreed criteria, becomes a change request.
06

What we need from DecentraLabs

The four-day timeline and the price assume you provide the following within one business day of the start, so the clock is not waiting on access.

  • A single named point of contact who can give approvals during the build window.
  • Export or admin access to your current CRMs (Iron Forge and GoHighLevel) for leads, stages and payments.
  • Meta / Facebook Ads form and webhook access.
  • Final stage names and your exact KPI definitions, confirmed against Section 02.
  • Team list with roles (Admin / Closer / Setter).
  • The subdomain to host on, the VPS, and the dialer and WhatsApp numbers.

Delays in providing access push the timeline out and may affect the price.

07

Tools & running costs

  • What we build with: a production stack with a relational database, secure role-based auth and API-first integrations. No spreadsheets behind the scenes.
  • Third-party costs are yours: hosting / VPS, the dialer and call-recording service, the WhatsApp number and any AI transcription usage are billed directly to DecentraLabs, not included in the build price. We confirm the exact costs before any integration proceeds.
  • Call transcripts are AI-generated and may contain errors. They are a convenience, not a verbatim legal record, and should be reviewed before being relied on.
08

Changes to scope

Any change to scope, deliverables, timeline or price is written down in a change request and signed by both sides before that work starts. Additional work outside the included revisions is quoted before it begins. Nothing verbal is binding.
